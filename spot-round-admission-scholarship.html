Do You Get a Scholarship in Spot Round Admission? No.

Expert answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET counsellor in Pune: no scholarship for spot round admissions. How spot rounds work, the official scholarship rule, and what it costs at Pune colleges.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: COEP Tech scholarship instructions (official PDF) · MahaDBT portal · FRA-approved fees 2026-27 (our fee table)✅ Free · no login

Do we get a scholarship if we take admission in a spot round?

No. A student admitted through a spot round gets no scholarship and no freeship — whatever the category (SC, OBC, VJNT, SBC, SEBC, EWS or Open). You pay the full Open-category fee of that college. In an ILS (institute-level) round the fee is about three times higher.

Answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET admission counsellor, Baner, Pune — 7 years of MHT-CET counselling (since 2020). Backed by COEP Technological University's official scholarship instructions (see below).

Quick answer

If you take admission through a spot round (an “Against-CAP” seat), you get no scholarship and pay the full Open-category fee — whatever your category. COEP Technological University's official scholarship instructions say there is no Government scholarship for SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC and EWS students admitted through Against-CAP / spot round, citing a government MahaDBT letter dated 5 December 2023. The same document lists the Open/EWS fee scholarship only for students admitted through the CAP round, and says TFWS students get no other scholarship.

At a Pune private college that means about ₹2,06,000 a year — ₹8,24,000 over four years (VIT, Bibwewadi, 2026-27 approved fee), paid by your family instead of being reimbursed.

Every year, after CAP Round 4, parents ask me two things: “what is a spot round?” and “will we still get the scholarship if we take a seat there?”. This page answers both — the spot round process the way I explain it to my students, and the scholarship rule from an official college document, with real fee numbers so you can decide before you go.

What is a spot round?

A spot round is a college-level round to fill seats that are still vacant after all four CAP rounds. Seats fall vacant because some students join IIT, NIT or IIIT, some move to medical, some had only blocked a seat — and because of a chain effect: if PICT has a few vacant seats, students from VIT or PCCOE move up to PICT, which opens seats at VIT, and so on down the list.

  • Ignore rumours. Every year people claim “spot admissions have started” or offer to “arrange” a seat. Spot rounds happen only after the official CAP rounds are over, on dates the college announces.
  • Vacancy list: after CAP Round 4 reporting closes, a PDF shows the vacant seats in every college, category-wise. Colleges hold their spot rounds based on it.
  • Registration is compulsory. You cannot just walk in. Register for that college's spot / institute-level round either on the college's own website or through the CET Cell portal (after login, the institute-level form option). It is free — register early for every college you would consider, because the vacancy PDF comes very late.
  • Be there in person. On the announced day (for example “reporting 8 am”) the student must be physically present.
  • Merit order, branch by branch. The college shows the vacant seats (for example CS 10, IT 5, E&TC 6). Registered students who are present are called in merit order; the first student picks a branch, then the next, until the seats are gone.
  • Admission on the spot. You pay the fee — the Open-category fee, whatever your category — and submit documents there and then — that is why it is called a “spot” round.

For the full list of round types (institutional, ACAP, spot) see spot round and ACAP explained.

Spot round vs ILS (institute-level) round

Don't confuse the two. Many colleges hold a normal spot round and a separate institute-level (ILS) round. The process is similar, but the ILS fee is about three times the normal fee — and there is no scholarship on it either. In the last season, for example, VIT held its spot round at the main campus and its ILS round at its Kondhwa campus. Always ask which round a seat is from before paying.

Got a seat in CAP Round 4? Keep it

If you were allotted a college in CAP Round 4 and want to try a better college in spot rounds, first confirm the Round 4 seat: take admission, pay the fee and submit documents. You get a receipt saying the college holds your documents. At a spot round, show that receipt — the new college admits you on it and usually gives two to three days for the remaining documents. In my experience the first college then refunds the college fee you paid and returns your documents. Check the cancellation and refund rules in the official CAP information brochure for your year.

But read the next section before you switch — moving from a CAP seat to a spot seat can cost you your scholarship.

Scholarship rule for spot round admissions

Based on COEP Technological University's official “Scholarship Information” instructions, which say there is no Government scholarship for SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC and EWS students admitted through “Against-CAP / SPOT Round” (citing a MahaDBT letter dated 05/12/2023), and on Yash Aradhye's experience that every spot-round student pays the Open-category fee:

Admission route / seatScholarship?
Spot round / Against-CAP — any categoryNo scholarship, no freeship. Pay the full Open-category fee
ILS (institute-level) roundNo. Fee about 3× the normal fee
CAP round — SC, STYes: scholarship (income below ₹2.5 lakh) or freeship (above ₹2.5 lakh) — tuition and exam fees
CAP round — OBC, VJNT, SBCYes: scholarship (income below ₹1.5 lakh) or freeship (₹1.5–8 lakh) — tuition and exam fees
CAP round — Open / EWSYes: Rajarshi Shahu scholarship, 50% of tuition and exam fees (income below ₹8 lakh)
CAP round — minority communitiesYes: ₹25,000 or actual course fee (income below ₹8 lakh)
TFWS seatFee waiver only; no other scholarship. Income below ₹8 lakh: Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh hostel allowance

In practice: a student admitted through a spot round gets no scholarship or freeship and pays the full Open-category fee of that college, whatever their category. Scholarship applications for CAP-round admissions are made on the MahaDBT portal, usually within one month of final admission.

How to decide

  • If your CAP seat is in a scholarship-eligible category, compare the full Open fee of the spot seat over four years with what the better college really gives you.
  • Register for spot rounds anyway — it is free and keeps your options open; decide on the day with the numbers in front of you.
  • For a TFWS seat, remember the fee waiver itself is tied to that seat.

Frequently asked questions

Do we get a scholarship for spot round admission?

No. Answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET counsellor, Pune: a student admitted through a spot round gets no scholarship and no freeship, whatever the category, and pays the full Open-category fee of that college. In an ILS round the fee is about three times higher.

Do students admitted in a spot round get a scholarship in Maharashtra?

No. COEP Technological University's official scholarship instructions say there is no Government scholarship for SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC and EWS students admitted through Against-CAP / spot round, citing a MahaDBT letter dated 5 December 2023. The Open/EWS Rajarshi Shahu scholarship is listed only for students admitted through the CAP round. A student admitted in a spot round pays the full Open-category fee, whatever their category.

Which fee do I pay if I get a seat in a spot round?

The full Open-category fee of that college, whatever your category. There is no scholarship or freeship for a spot-round admission.

I am OBC / SC / VJNT. What do I lose if I leave my CAP seat for a spot seat?

Through a CAP seat, eligible SC, ST, OBC, VJNT and SBC students get tuition and exam fees covered by a MahaDBT scholarship or freeship (depending on family income). Through a spot seat you get no scholarship and pay the full Open-category fee for all four years.

Does an EWS student get a scholarship after spot round admission?

No. EWS is one of the categories named in COEP's official instructions as getting no Government scholarship when admitted through Against-CAP / spot round.

Does an Open-category student get the Rajarshi Shahu (EBC) scholarship in a spot round?

COEP's document lists the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojana (50% of tuition and exam fees, family income below ₹8 lakh) only for students admitted through the CAP round. A spot-round student pays the full Open-category fee.

How much more will I pay at a Pune college through a spot seat?

At 2026-27 FRA-approved fees, the Open-category fee is ₹1,25,500 a year at PICT (₹5,02,000 over four years) and ₹2,06,000 a year at VIT Bibwewadi (₹8,24,000 over four years). Through a spot seat your family pays this in full; through a CAP seat an eligible student gets tuition and exam fees (or 50% of them for Open/EWS) reimbursed.

Is the spot round the same as an Against-CAP admission for scholarships?

Yes, for scholarship purposes COEP's official document names them together: “Against-CAP / SPOT Round”. Neither gets a Government scholarship.

Is the ILS round fee the same as the spot round fee?

No. An institute-level (ILS) seat costs about three times the normal fee, according to Yash Aradhye, and gets no scholarship. Many colleges hold both rounds separately, so ask which round a seat is from before you pay.

Do TFWS students get a scholarship?

According to COEP's scholarship document, no other Government scholarship applies to TFWS students; those with family income below ₹8 lakh may apply for the Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh hostel allowance (Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojana).

What are the income limits for scholarship and freeship through a CAP seat?

As listed by COEP: SC and ST — scholarship below ₹2.5 lakh, freeship (tuition and exam fees) above ₹2.5 lakh with no upper limit; OBC, VJNT and SBC — scholarship below ₹1.5 lakh, freeship between ₹1.5 lakh and ₹8 lakh; Open/EWS — 50% of tuition and exam fees below ₹8 lakh; minority communities — ₹25,000 or actual course fee below ₹8 lakh.

Where and when do I apply for the scholarship after a CAP admission?

On the MahaDBT portal, within one month of the date of your final admission (as per COEP's instructions). If the application is incomplete, the student and parents are held responsible for losing the scholarship, so finish every step.

What about All India quota and J&K students?

COEP's document says All India quota students apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), and J&K / PMSSS category students apply on the AICTE J&K PMSSS portal.

What is a spot round in MHT-CET admission?

A college-level round held after the four CAP rounds to fill seats that are still vacant. You register in advance, attend in person on the announced day and are called in merit order; fees and documents are taken on the spot, which is why it is called a spot round.

Why are seats vacant after four CAP rounds?

Some students join IIT, NIT or IIIT, some move to medical, some had only blocked a seat. There is also a chain effect: when PICT has vacancies, students from VIT or PCCOE move up, which opens seats at VIT, and so on.

When do spot rounds happen?

Only after the official CAP rounds are over. After CAP Round 4 reporting closes, a PDF shows category-wise vacant seats in every college, and each college announces its own spot round date. Ignore anyone who says spot admissions have “already started” before that.

How do I register for a spot round?

Either on the college's own website or through the CET Cell portal — after login, use the institute-level form option and select the college. Registration is free. Register early for every college you would consider, because the vacancy PDF comes very late.

Can I attend a spot round without registering?

No. Merit for the spot round is made only from registered students. If you have not registered, going to the college on that day is of no use.

Does the student have to be present in person?

Yes. On the announced day (for example “reporting 8 am”) the student must be physically present at the college.

How are seats given in a spot round?

The college displays its vacant seats branch-wise (for example CS 10, IT 5, E&TC 6). Registered students who are present are seated and called in merit order. The top student picks a branch, then the next, until the seats are filled.

Can an agent “arrange” a spot round seat for me?

No. Spot rounds are held on announced dates in merit order among registered students who are present. Do not pay anyone who promises a seat.

Should I confirm my CAP Round 4 seat before going to spot rounds?

Yes. Take admission at the Round 4 college, pay the fee, submit documents and keep the receipt. Remember that moving to a spot seat means no scholarship and the full Open-category fee.

Which documents do I need at the spot round if my documents are with another college?

Show the admission receipt from your current college that says it holds your documents. The new college admits you on that receipt and usually gives two to three days for the remaining documents.

Will I get my fees back if I move from my CAP college to a spot round college?

In Yash Aradhye's experience, the first college refunds the college fee you paid and returns your documents. Check the cancellation and refund rules in the official CAP information brochure for your year.

As a reserved-category student, should I still register for spot rounds?

Yes — registration is free and keeps your options open. On the day, compare four years of the full Open-category fee with what the better college really gives you before you accept.

स्पॉट राऊंडमधून ऍडमिशन घेतल्यास स्कॉलरशिप मिळते का?

नाही. COEP च्या अधिकृत माहितीनुसार Against-CAP / स्पॉट राऊंडमधून ऍडमिशन घेतलेल्या SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC, EWS विद्यार्थ्यांना सरकारी स्कॉलरशिप मिळत नाही. कुठल्याही कॅटेगरीचा विद्यार्थी असला तरी ओपन कॅटेगरीची पूर्ण फी भरावी लागते.

स्पॉट राऊंडसाठी रजिस्ट्रेशन कसे करायचे?

कॉलेजच्या वेबसाईटवर किंवा CET Cell पोर्टलवर लॉगिन करून institute-level फॉर्ममधून. रजिस्ट्रेशन फ्री आहे. Vacancy PDF उशिरा येते, म्हणून आधीच रजिस्ट्रेशन करून ठेवा.

स्पॉट राऊंडला विद्यार्थ्याने स्वतः जावे लागते का?

हो. ठरलेल्या दिवशी विद्यार्थ्याने कॉलेजमध्ये प्रत्यक्ष हजर राहणे बंधनकारक आहे. मेरिटनुसार बोलावले जाते आणि फी व डॉक्युमेंट्स तिथेच द्यावे लागतात.

CAP Round 4 ची सीट सोडून स्पॉट राऊंडला जावे का?

आधी Round 4 ची सीट कन्फर्म करा, फी भरा आणि रिसीट जपून ठेवा. स्पॉट सीट घेतल्यास स्कॉलरशिप मिळणार नाही आणि चार वर्षे ओपनची पूर्ण फी भरावी लागेल — हे लक्षात घेऊनच निर्णय घ्या.
