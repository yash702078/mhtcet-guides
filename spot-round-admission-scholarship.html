Spot Round Admission and Scholarship: Will You Still Get It?

How MHT-CET spot rounds work, why a spot-round seat usually means no Government scholarship, and how much that can cost at Pune colleges — with official fee numbers.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: COEP Tech scholarship instructions (official PDF) · MahaDBT portal · FRA-approved fees 2026-27 (our fee table)✅ Free · no login
Quick answer

If you take admission through a spot round (an “Against-CAP” seat), you get no scholarship and pay the full Open-category fee — whatever your category. COEP Technological University's official scholarship instructions say there is no Government scholarship for SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC and EWS students admitted through Against-CAP / spot round, citing a government MahaDBT letter dated 5 December 2023. The same document lists the Open/EWS fee scholarship only for students admitted through the CAP round, and says TFWS students get no other scholarship.

At a Pune private college that means about ₹2,06,000 a year — ₹8,24,000 over four years (VIT, Bibwewadi, 2026-27 approved fee), paid by your family instead of being reimbursed.

Every year, after CAP Round 4, parents ask me two things: “what is a spot round?” and “will we still get the scholarship if we take a seat there?”. This page answers both — the spot round process the way I explain it to my students, and the scholarship rule from an official college document, with real fee numbers so you can decide before you go.

What is a spot round?

A spot round is a college-level round to fill seats that are still vacant after all four CAP rounds. Seats fall vacant because some students join IIT, NIT or IIIT, some move to medical, some had only blocked a seat — and because of a chain effect: if PICT has a few vacant seats, students from VIT or PCCOE move up to PICT, which opens seats at VIT, and so on down the list.

  • Ignore rumours. Every year people claim “spot admissions have started” or offer to “arrange” a seat. Spot rounds happen only after the official CAP rounds are over, on dates the college announces.
  • Vacancy list: after CAP Round 4 reporting closes, a PDF shows the vacant seats in every college, category-wise. Colleges hold their spot rounds based on it.
  • Registration is compulsory. You cannot just walk in. Register for that college's spot / institute-level round either on the college's own website or through the CET Cell portal (after login, the institute-level form option). It is free — register early for every college you would consider, because the vacancy PDF comes very late.
  • Be there in person. On the announced day (for example “reporting 8 am”) the student must be physically present.
  • Merit order, branch by branch. The college shows the vacant seats (for example CS 10, IT 5, E&TC 6). Registered students who are present are called in merit order; the first student picks a branch, then the next, until the seats are gone.
  • Admission on the spot. You pay the fee — the Open-category fee, whatever your category — and submit documents there and then — that is why it is called a “spot” round.

For the full list of round types (institutional, ACAP, spot) see spot round and ACAP explained.

Spot round vs ILS (institute-level) round

Don't confuse the two. Many colleges hold a normal spot round and a separate institute-level (ILS) round. The process is similar, but the ILS fee is higher than the normal fee. In the last season, for example, VIT held its spot round at the main campus and its ILS round at its Kondhwa campus. Always ask which round a seat is from before paying.

Got a seat in CAP Round 4? Keep it

If you were allotted a college in CAP Round 4 and want to try a better college in spot rounds, first confirm the Round 4 seat: take admission, pay the fee and submit documents. You get a receipt saying the college holds your documents. At a spot round, show that receipt — the new college admits you on it and usually gives two to three days for the remaining documents. In my experience the first college then refunds the college fee you paid and returns your documents. Check the cancellation and refund rules in the official CAP information brochure for your year.

But read the next section before you switch — moving from a CAP seat to a spot seat can cost you your scholarship.

Scholarship rule for spot round admissions

From COEP Technological University's official “Scholarship Information” instructions:

Admission route / seatWhat the document says
Against-CAP / spot round — SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC, EWSNo Government scholarship (cites MahaDBT letter dated 05/12/2023)
CAP round — SC, STScholarship (income below ₹2.5 lakh) or freeship (above ₹2.5 lakh): tuition and exam fees
CAP round — OBC, VJNT, SBCScholarship (income below ₹1.5 lakh) or freeship (₹1.5–8 lakh): tuition and exam fees
CAP round — Open / EWSRajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojana: 50% of tuition and exam fees, income below ₹8 lakh — listed for students admitted through the CAP round
CAP round — minority communitiesDTE minority scholarship: ₹25,000 or actual course fee, income below ₹8 lakh
TFWS seatNo other Government scholarship; students with income below ₹8 lakh may apply for the Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh hostel allowance (Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta)

In practice: a student admitted through a spot round gets no scholarship or freeship and pays the full Open-category fee of that college, whatever their category. Scholarship applications for CAP-round admissions are made on the MahaDBT portal, usually within one month of final admission.

How to decide

  • If your CAP seat is in a scholarship-eligible category, compare the full Open fee of the spot seat over four years with what the better college really gives you.
  • Register for spot rounds anyway — it is free and keeps your options open; decide on the day with the numbers in front of you.
  • For a TFWS seat, remember the fee waiver itself is tied to that seat.

CAP Round 4 ला सीट मिळाली असेल तर ती आधी कन्फर्म करा, फी भरा, डॉक्युमेंट्स द्या आणि रिसीट जपून ठेवा.

Frequently asked questions

Do students admitted in a spot round get a scholarship in Maharashtra?

COEP Technological University's official scholarship instructions say there is no Government scholarship for SC, VJNT, OBC, SBC, SEBC and EWS students admitted through Against-CAP / spot round, citing a MahaDBT letter dated 5 December 2023. The Open/EWS Rajarshi Shahu scholarship is listed for students admitted through the CAP round. A student admitted in a spot round pays the full Open-category fee, whatever their category.

What is a spot round in MHT-CET admission?

A college-level round held after the four CAP rounds to fill vacant seats. You must register (college website or CET Cell portal), be present in person on the announced day, and are called in merit order; fees and documents are taken on the spot.

Should I confirm my CAP Round 4 seat before going to spot rounds?

Yes. Take admission at the Round 4 college and keep the receipt. If you get a better seat in a spot round, the new college admits you on that receipt. But remember that a spot seat can mean losing the scholarship a CAP seat would have given you.

Is the ILS round fee the same as the spot round fee?

No. Institute-level (ILS) seats carry a higher fee than normal spot round seats. Ask which round a seat is from before you pay.

Do TFWS students get a scholarship?

According to COEP's scholarship document, no other Government scholarship applies to TFWS students; those with family income below ₹8 lakh may apply for the Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh hostel allowance.
