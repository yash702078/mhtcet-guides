Will the JEE Main 2027 Cutoff Increase or Decrease? Registrations, Past Cutoffs and Our Estimate

JEE Main registrations are up again — will the cutoff go up and make JEE Advanced harder to reach?

Three years of qualifying cutoffs, the 2027 registration trend, the simple maths behind cutoffs, and a category-wise estimate you can check with a calculator.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 24 Nov 2026
📄 Source: NTA, JEE (Main) 2026 Paper 1 result notice (20 Apr 2026) · NTA, JEE Main 2027 Session 1 registration update (23 Nov 2026)✅ Free · no login
Yash's answer

If more students register this year than last year, cutoffs will go up and getting a college becomes more difficult. If fewer students register, admission can become a little easier.

Yash Aradhye, 24 Nov 2026

Quick answer

More likely to increase. JEE Main 2027 Session 1 already has 13,92,344 fee-paid candidates (15,69,514 forms; NTA, 23 Nov 2026) — about 3.1% more than the 13,50,969 scheduled for Session 1 in 2026. Only the top 2,50,000 qualify for JEE Advanced, so more candidates means a higher percentile is needed. At +3%, our estimate for the General cutoff is about 93.60 (2026: 93.41). This is an estimate, not an official number — NTA announces the real cutoff with the Session 2 result in April 2027.

“Will the JEE Main cutoff go up this year?” is the most asked question after registrations close. Most pages answer with a guess. This page uses three years of official qualifying cutoffs and the registration trend, explains the simple maths behind cutoffs, and gives an estimate for every category plus a calculator.

Why more registrations push the cutoff up

The number of JEE Advanced slots is fixed at 2,50,000 (split by category). A percentile tells you what share of candidates scored below you. If more students write JEE Main but the slots stay the same, the top 2,50,000 become a smaller share of everyone — so the percentile of the last qualifier goes up.

Check with real numbers: Session 1 candidates grew about 3.0% from 2025 (13,11,544, as reported) to 2026 (13,50,969). The General cutoff rose from 93.10 to 93.41, which matches about 4.7% more candidates in our formula. So registrations explain most of last year's rise. Paper difficulty and normalisation add some noise every year.

Not just the qualifying cutoff: NIT and Maharashtra seats

  • NIT, IIIT and GFTI admission is by rank, not percentile. With more candidates, the same percentile gives a worse rank. If the rank you need for a branch stays the same, you need a slightly higher percentile. Dates and rounds: JoSAA 2027 counselling.
  • Maharashtra: JEE Main is also used for the All India seats in Maharashtra's CAP — see engineering admission through JEE Main in Maharashtra. For MHT-CET the same logic applies: will MHT-CET 2027 cutoffs rise?

What this estimate does not know

  • Final registration (late fee window closes 26 Nov 2026) and Session 2 numbers are not out yet; many students write both sessions.
  • Paper difficulty and NTA's normalisation change the score-to-percentile curve each year.
  • The official cutoff comes only with the Session 2 result (April 2027). We will update this page with the final registration count and the official cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

Will the JEE Main 2027 cutoff increase?

Likely yes. Fee-paid registrations for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 (13,92,344) are about 3.1% above the 2026 Session 1 count. With JEE Advanced slots fixed at 2,50,000, more candidates push the qualifying percentile up. At +3% our estimate for General is about 93.60 (2026: 93.41). This is an estimate; NTA announces the official cutoff in April 2027.

What was the JEE Main 2026 cutoff for JEE Advanced?

As per NTA's 2026 Paper 1 result: General (CRL) 93.4123549, EWS 82.4164528, OBC-NCL 80.9232583, SC 63.9172792 and ST 52.0174712.

Did the JEE Main cutoff increase in 2026 compared to 2025?

Yes, in every category. General went from 93.10 to 93.41, OBC-NCL from 79.43 to 80.92, SC from 61.15 to 63.92 and ST from 47.90 to 52.02.

If fewer students register, will the cutoff fall?

Yes, a little. With fewer candidates and the same 2,50,000 slots, the last qualifier's percentile is lower.

How many students registered for JEE Main 2027?

As per NTA's post of 23 November 2026, 15,69,514 forms were submitted for Session 1 and 13,92,344 candidates had paid the fee. The last date with late fee is 26 November 2026.

When will the JEE Main 2027 cutoff be announced?

NTA announces the JEE Advanced qualifying cutoff with the final Paper 1 result after Session 2. In 2026 this was on 20 April; JEE Advanced 2027 is on 23 May 2027.
