Saarthi Plus: what our team does for you, step by step

From the first registration to the spot round, the team does the work with you. You stay at home, you approve every decision, and nothing is submitted without your OTP.

  1. 📝 CET Cell registration — done for you, online

    Many students make mistakes at registration. We register you on the CET Cell portal while you stay at home.

    रजिस्ट्रेशन आम्ही करून देऊ — घरबसल्या.

  2. 📄 Documents on WhatsApp — we upload and get them verified

    Send your documents on WhatsApp. We upload them and complete the verification step.

    डॉक्युमेंट्स WhatsApp वर पाठवा, अपलोड आणि व्हेरिफिकेशन आम्ही करू.

  3. 🧩 Your college list — built with you in a meeting

    We sit with you (online or at Baner) and build a proper college list for your percentile, category and goals.

  4. ⏳ Time to think, then revise together

    You get enough time to study the list. Any change is discussed again together. The final list is ready before CAP Round 1 — college names are entered only in the CAP rounds, not at registration.

  5. ⚡ CAP Round 1 filled on priority

    Round 1 gives only about three days, and the portal often slows down or OTPs come late. Our team fills your form on priority — no cyber-café queue.

    Day 1Window opens
    Day 2⚠️ Server slow
    Day 3⚠️ OTP delays
    ✅ Your list is ready and filed early

  6. 🎥 Google Meet check before submitting

    After filling, we show you the filled form on Google Meet so you can confirm it matches the list we agreed. It is submitted only after you give the OTP and the green signal.

    तुमच्या OTP आणि ग्रीन सिग्नलशिवाय काहीही सबमिट होत नाही.

  7. 🔄 All four CAP rounds — freeze or betterment, decided with you

    We fill the form for every round. After each allotment we explain whether to freeze or go for betterment, and we fill the betterment form too.

  8. 📍 Spot rounds — where, when, how many seats

    Which college is holding a spot round, when, how many vacancies there are and where you should go — we track it and tell you.

18 FAQs about Saarthi counselling

Plans and price
What is Saarthi counselling?
Saarthi is the MHT-CET engineering admission counselling programme run by Yash Aradhye (Yash Dada) from Baner, Pune, available online across Maharashtra and in person.
How much do Saarthi and Saarthi Plus cost?
Saarthi costs ₹2,399 and Saarthi Plus costs ₹6,999. Both are one-time payments covering all CAP rounds.
What is the difference between Saarthi and Saarthi Plus?
Saarthi is guided self-service: personalised college selection, strategy, a choice-filling guide and unlimited WhatsApp support. Saarthi Plus is done-for-you: the team handles registration, documents, builds the list with you, fills the option form with your consent, monitors rounds, helps with spot rounds, and gives you a dedicated counsellor.
How do I pay and enrol?
Use the secure Razorpay buttons on this page. After payment you are added to your batch and contacted by the team.
Are seats limited?
Yes. Batches are kept small so every student gets personal replies. RG Lectures noted the 2024 batch filled within 20 minutes.
Saarthi Plus process
Do I need to go to a cyber café to fill the CAP option form?
Not with Saarthi Plus. The team fills your form online on priority from the start of each round, so you avoid last-day server slowdowns and OTP delays. With Saarthi you fill it yourself using our step-by-step guide.
Will my option form be submitted without my approval?
No. After filling, we show you the filled form on Google Meet so you can check it matches the list we agreed. It is submitted only after you give the OTP and your go-ahead.
Which category benefits do you consider while making my list?
Every benefit you are eligible for — Home University seats, your reserved category if applicable, and minority quota where it applies — so your percentile reaches the best possible college.
Do you help with spot rounds, institute-level (ILS) and management quota seats?
Yes. We tell Saarthi Plus students which colleges are holding spot rounds, when, how many vacancies there are and where to go, and we explain institute-level and management-quota options with their real costs. We never promise a seat for money.
How it works
Is counselling online or offline?
Both. You can meet the team at the Baner, Pune office or get counselling online through video calls and WhatsApp anywhere in Maharashtra.
Who fills the CAP option form in Saarthi Plus?
The professional team fills it after building the list with you, and takes your consent for every college and branch. You keep control of the OTP.
Does Saarthi cover spot rounds?
Saarthi Plus includes active spot-round participation guidance. Saarthi covers the CAP rounds.
Does Saarthi help with JEE-based and category-based admission?
Yes — including All-India seats via JEE Main, SEBC/EWS/OBC category questions and the girls' fee-waiver documents.
Can Saarthi guarantee a particular college?
No honest counsellor can guarantee a college. Saarthi maximises what your rank can get through correct documents, a well-ordered list and round-by-round decisions.
Does Saarthi arrange management-quota or paid seats?
Saarthi focuses on the merit route first. Management quota is discussed honestly as an option, never as a 'seat for cash' promise.
