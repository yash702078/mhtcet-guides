Does the MHT-CET Cutoff Drop After Round 1? 2026 Data Measured

Official CAP 2026 Round I–IV closing percentiles compared for every Open-category branch seat — so you can decide between freeze and betterment with real numbers.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: Yash Aradhye videos · State CET Cell CAP 2026 lists✅ Free · no login

Does the cutoff drop after CAP Round 1?

For good colleges, very little. In 2026 the median Round 1→2 drop was only 0.02 percentile for colleges that closed at 99+ and 0.01 for 97–99. And in later rounds many cutoffs went up, not down. So keep your seat with betterment instead of hoping for a big drop.

Data: official State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists, 2,327 Open-category (state-level and home-university) branch seats, analysed by Yash Aradhye's team. Advice: Answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET admission counsellor, Baner, Pune — 7 years of MHT-CET counselling (since 2020). What it means for your decision

  • If you are within a few tenths of a percentile of a better college, betterment gives you a real chance — and costs you nothing if you have paid the seat acceptance fee.
  • If you are far below, don't give up a seat hoping for a big drop. It mostly doesn't happen at the top.

Frequently asked questions

Does the MHT-CET cutoff drop in CAP Round 2?

Usually a little. In 2026 the median Round 1→2 drop for Open seats was 0.02 percentile for colleges that closed at 99+, 0.01 for 97–99 and 2.47 for colleges below 80.

Does the cutoff always go down from Round 1 to Round 4?

No. In 2026 the last round closed higher than Round 1 for 23% of seats at 99+ and for 71% of seats below 80. Later rounds fill only vacated seats, so the closing percentile can go up.

Why does the cutoff go up in later rounds?

The closing percentile is the percentile of the last student allotted in that round. In later rounds only vacated seats are filled, often by students moving up through betterment, so the last allotted student can have a higher percentile than in Round 1.

Should I wait for Round 3 or 4 to get a top college?

For top colleges the change after Round 1 is very small. Keep your seat with betterment and fill only better options, instead of giving up a seat and waiting.
