Engineering Admission Counselling in Maharashtra (MHT-CET 2027): Do It Yourself or Take Expert Help?

Should I do CAP myself or take counselling? What if I make a mistake?

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 28 Nov 2026
📄 Source: CET Cell Information Brochure 2026-27 · State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Yash's answer

Every student is unique. Marks differ, the home district differs (Nagpur, Latur, Nanded, Satara, Sangli…), and goals differ: one student wants a studious college, another sports, UPSC/MPSC preparation or placements. That is why a college list has to be personal. Good counselling aims for the best possible college for your percentile by using every benefit you have: Home University, your reserved category, or minority quota.

— Yash Aradhye, from his Saarthi Plus video. 7 years of MHT-CET counselling (since 2020).

Quick answer
  • MHT-CET counselling (also spelled “counseling”) means the official CAP (Centralised Admission Process) run by the State CET Cell: registration, document verification, merit list, then up to 4 CAP rounds of option forms and seat allotment. The official process itself is online, and the steps are the same for every student.
  • You can do it yourself if you read every notice, fill the option form carefully in the short window, and understand freeze, betterment and seat acceptance.
  • Personal counselling (a counsellor like Yash's team) is for families who want help building the college list, using category and Home University benefits, and making the freeze/betterment decision each round.
  • What students lose seats over is rarely the percentile — it is small process mistakes (list below).

Why the college list is the hard part (CAP 2026 in numbers)

The official CAP 2026 cutoff lists (Round I–IV) that power our Cutoff Explorer contain:

  • 387 colleges and 2,333 college-branch choices,
  • 97 different seat/category codes (Open, OBC, SEBC, EWS, TFWS, ladies, Home University, Other-HU, PwD, defence, minority…),
  • the same branch closing at different percentiles for different seat types.

Home University matters, measured: in 1,261 college-branches that had both seat types in Round 1, the Open Other-University seat closed higher than the Open Home-University seat in 58% of cases, by a median of 2.29 percentile. So the same percentile can be enough at a college in your own university area and not enough elsewhere. This is the “category benefit” Yash talks about.

Cutoffs don't simply fall in later rounds: for 812 Open (State Level) seats, the last round closed higher than Round 1 in 70% of cases. Waiting for “the cutoff will drop” is risky — see how much cutoffs changed from Round 1 to Round 4.

Where students lose seats every year (Yash's experience)

  1. Clicking “Incorrect” at self-verification. It only asks whether the details you gave are correct. One “Incorrect” can cancel the seat.
  2. Not paying the ₹1,000 seat acceptance fee. Without it the status shows “Not confirmed” and the Round 1 seat is cancelled automatically — even if you chose betterment.
  3. Filling options worse than your current seat in Round 2. You hold only one allotment; if a lower option is allotted, the better seat is gone. (Round 2 option form guide)
  4. Leaving the option form to the last day. CAP Round 1 has only about 3 days, and the portal can go down or the OTP may not come.
  5. Believing “spot admission has started” rumours. Spot rounds start only after the official CAP rounds, and you must register for them in advance. (spot round guide)
  6. Not using category or Home University benefits when building the list.

Doing it yourself? A 7-point check

  • I know my candidature type, category and Home University.
  • My category documents (caste, validity, NCL / EWS, income) will be ready before registration.
  • I have a ranked list of colleges for my percentile, category and Home University — not just Open cutoffs.
  • I will fill the option form on day 1 of the window.
  • I understand freeze vs betterment and will pay the seat acceptance fee.
  • In Round 2+ I will put only options I truly prefer over my current seat.
  • I will register for institute-level/spot rounds of colleges I would consider.

If you can tick all seven, you can manage the CAP yourself. If not, get help early — the free 10-minute call below is a good first step.

Frequently asked questions

What is MHT-CET counselling?

It is the official Centralised Admission Process (CAP) of the State CET Cell, Maharashtra, for first-year engineering: online registration, document verification (e-scrutiny or Facilitation Centre), provisional and final merit lists, and up to 4 CAP rounds of option forms and seat allotment, followed by institute-level/spot rounds.

Is MHT-CET counseling free?

The official CAP is run by the State CET Cell; you pay only the official fees (the CAP registration fee and, after allotment, the seat acceptance fee — ₹1,000 as explained in Yash's CAP video). Private counselling is optional and paid; Yash Aradhye's Saarthi plans are ₹2,399 and ₹6,999.

Do I need a counsellor for engineering admission in Maharashtra?

Not necessarily. The steps are official and the same for everyone. Students lose seats mostly through process mistakes (self-verification, unpaid seat acceptance fee, wrong Round 2 options) and weak college lists. If you are unsure about these, get help before CAP Round 1.

How many CAP rounds are there in MHT-CET counselling?

In 2026 there were 4 CAP rounds for engineering, followed by institute-level and spot rounds for vacant seats.

What does Home University mean in CAP?

Many seats are reserved for candidates from the university area of the college (Home University). In the 2026 Round 1 lists, the Open Other-University seat closed higher than the Open Home-University seat in 58% of 1,261 college-branches (median 2.29 percentile).

When does MHT-CET counselling 2027 start?

Not announced yet. CAP starts after the MHT-CET 2027 result; the exam itself is tentatively in April–May 2027 (CET Cell, 16 Nov 2026). In 2026, CAP registration ran in July. Join the WhatsApp group to get the official date the day it is out.

Can a counsellor submit my option form without me?

In Saarthi Plus the team fills the form, shows the family the filled list on Google Meet, and submits only after the student gives the OTP and a green signal.
