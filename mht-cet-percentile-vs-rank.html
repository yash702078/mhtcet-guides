MHT-CET Percentile vs Rank: Official 2026 Data, Rules and How Percentile Is Calculated

Got your percentile but no idea what rank or college it means?

What your percentile means in rank (from real CAP 2026 merit data), which score counts if you write both attempts, and why marks-vs-percentile tables are only estimates.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 25 Nov 2026
📄 Source: State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists · MHT-CET 2026 information brochure (CET Cell) · CET Cell normalisation document (MH Nursing CET)✅ Free · no login
Quick answer (official 2026 data)

In the official MHT-CET CAP 2026 merit lists, 99 percentile was about rank 4,300, 98 percentile about 8,600, 95 percentile about 21,300, 90 percentile about 42,300 and 80 percentile about 83,000. Percentile is not percentage of marks: it tells you what share of students in your session scored the same as or less than you. If you write both attempts, only the better total percentile counts.

Every year after the exam, thousands of students search “how much percentile for X marks” and “what rank is 95 percentile”. Most pages online answer with guessed tables. This page uses only official sources: the CET Cell information brochure for the rules, and 82,242 real percentile–rank pairs from the official CAP 2026 Round 1–4 cutoff lists for the rank table. Official rules: percentile, two attempts, which score counts

From the State CET Cell information brochure for MHT-CET 2026:

  • “Percentile score is not the same as percentage of marks obtained.” (Section 8)
  • “Percentile scores will be calculated based on the raw (actual) marks obtained by each candidate.” (Section 10)
  • “If a candidate appears for both attempts, the best of the two percentile scores (Total score only) will be considered for the admission process.” The best score is taken from the total percentile, not subject-wise. (Section 10)
  • Exam: 150 multiple-choice questions in 180 minutes. Physics and Chemistry carry 1 mark per question, Mathematics 2 marks. No negative marking. Physics + Chemistry come first (90 minutes, then auto-submitted), then Mathematics (90 minutes). (Sections 7.1, 7.3)

How CET Cell calculates a percentile

CET Cell's normalisation document explains the method it uses for its computer-based CETs. The version available online is the one published for the MH Nursing CET; the brochure says the MHT-CET document is published on mahacet.org. In that method:

  • Percentile is calculated separately for each session: Percentile = 100 × (number of candidates in your session with a total raw score equal to or less than yours) ÷ (total candidates who appeared in that session).
  • The top scorer of every session gets 100 percentile, which is why 26 students scored 100 percentile in PCM Attempt 1 2026 (16 sessions).
  • Percentiles are calculated up to 7 decimal places to reduce ties, and the total percentile is not an average of the subject percentiles.

Why “marks vs percentile” tables online are only guesses

Because the percentile is calculated inside each session, the same marks give a different percentile in an easy shift than in a hard one. CET Cell does not publish a marks-to-percentile table. So any “120 marks = X percentile” table online is an estimate from students' reports. Plan with the percentile → rank → college chain above, which uses only official numbers. Frequently asked questions

What rank is 95 percentile in MHT-CET?

In the official CAP 2026 merit lists, 95 percentile was about rank 21,300. 99 percentile was about 4,300 and 90 percentile about 42,300.

Is percentile the same as percentage in MHT-CET?

No. The CET Cell brochure says: “Percentile score is not the same as percentage of marks obtained.” Percentile shows what share of students in your session scored the same as or less than you.

If I write both MHT-CET attempts, which percentile counts?

The better of the two total percentiles. The CET Cell brochure says the best score is taken only from the total percentile, not from subject-wise percentiles.

Is there negative marking in MHT-CET?

No. As per the CET Cell brochure there is no negative marking. Physics and Chemistry carry 1 mark per question and Mathematics 2 marks; 150 questions in 180 minutes.

Why do so many students get 100 percentile?

Percentile is calculated separately for each session, and the top scorer of each session gets 100. In 2026, 26 candidates scored 100 percentile in PCM Attempt 1 and 12 in Attempt 2 (CET Cell press notes).

Is there an official MHT-CET marks vs percentile table?

No. CET Cell does not publish one, because the same marks give different percentiles in different sessions. Use the official percentile → rank data on this page instead.
