What is TFWS in MHT-CET CAP? Rules + how hard TFWS seats really are (2026 data)

Is TFWS an easy free seat? Am I even eligible?

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 26 Nov 2026
📄 Source: CET Cell Information Brochure 2026-27, Section 7(5)(c) · State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Short answer

TFWS is not a separate category like OBC or SC. It is a few extra seats (up to 5% per course) where the tuition fee is waived, for Maharashtra State candidates whose parents' annual income is below ₹8 lakh. Students of every category can apply. But it is not an easy seat: in our 2026 CAP data the TFWS seat closed above the Open cutoff of the same branch in 83% of cases. So if you are eligible, apply for TFWS — and still put the normal seats of the same colleges in your option form.

Key facts (official rules + 2026 data)
  • Who: only Maharashtra State Candidature candidates, parents' income from all sources below ₹8 lakh a year, income certificate from the appropriate authority.
  • Seats: up to 5% of sanctioned intake per course, supernumerary (over and above the normal seats), only in courses that filled at least 50% of approved intake the previous year.
  • Fee: only the tuition fee is waived. All other fees (development fee, exam fee, hostel etc.) are paid by the student.
  • Catch: a TFWS student cannot change institute or course at any stage.
  • Measured 2026: TFWS closed a median +1.85 percentile vs Open across 939 college-branches (Round 1).

Most TFWS pages repeat the rules and then say “TFWS cutoff is lower”. We checked it against the official MHT-CET CAP 2026 Round I–IV lists: 1,971 TFWS cutoff rows in 369 colleges. For 939 branches we could compare the Round 1 TFWS cutoff with the Open (State Level) cutoff of the very same branch. The result is the opposite of what many students think.

Reason: there are very few TFWS seats per branch, and every category competes for them on one merit list, so the last TFWS seat goes to a strong student. TFWS saves fees — it is not a shortcut to a better college.

Do TFWS cutoffs drop in later CAP rounds?

Comparing Round 1 with the last 2026 round in which each TFWS seat was allotted: the cutoff fell in 48% of branches, rose in 48%, and 17% of TFWS seats were allotted only in Round 1 (none left later). Do not wait for later rounds hoping TFWS gets easier — and remember the lock-in: once you take a TFWS seat you cannot change college or course.

What to do

  • Check income first: parents' income from all sources below ₹8 lakh, and get the income certificate from the appropriate authority early.
  • In the option form, list the normal seat choices you want; TFWS is an extra chance at the same college, not a replacement for your category seat.

मराठीत थोडक्यात

TFWS म्हणजे वेगळी कॅटेगरी नाही. प्रत्येक कोर्समध्ये जास्तीत जास्त 5% अतिरिक्त जागा, जिथे फक्त ट्यूशन फी माफ होते. पात्रता: महाराष्ट्र राज्य उमेदवार, पालकांचे सर्व स्रोतांतून वार्षिक उत्पन्न ₹8 लाखांपेक्षा कमी. बाकी फी विद्यार्थ्याने भरायची. TFWS जागा घेतल्यावर कॉलेज किंवा कोर्स बदलता येत नाही.

2026 च्या CAP डेटानुसार 83% ब्रांचमध्ये TFWS चा कटऑफ Open पेक्षा जास्त होता. त्यामुळे पात्र असाल तर TFWS साठी अर्ज करा, पण ऑप्शन फॉर्ममध्ये नेहमीच्या जागाही ठेवा.

Frequently asked questions

What is TFWS in MHT-CET CAP?

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) gives up to 5% extra (supernumerary) seats per course in which the tuition fee is waived. It is for Maharashtra State Candidature candidates whose parents' annual income from all sources is below ₹8 lakh (CET Cell brochure 2026-27, Section 7(5)(c)).

Is TFWS cutoff lower than Open?

Usually not. In the official CAP 2026 Round 1 lists, TFWS closed above the Open (State Level) cutoff of the same branch in 83% of 939 branches; the median gap was +1.85 percentile.

Can OBC, SC or EWS students apply for TFWS?

Yes. The brochure rule does not restrict TFWS by category: any Maharashtra State Candidature candidate within the ₹8 lakh income limit is eligible, and TFWS has one merit list.

Which fees are waived under TFWS?

Only the tuition fee (as approved by the Fee Regulating Authority or the Government). All other fees must be paid by the student.

Can I change college or branch after taking a TFWS seat?

No. The brochure says candidates admitted under TFWS shall not be allowed to change institution or course at any stage under any circumstances.

Does TFWS cutoff drop in later CAP rounds?

Sometimes, but not reliably: from Round 1 to the last round it fell in 48% of branches and rose in 48%; 17% of TFWS seats were allotted only in Round 1.
