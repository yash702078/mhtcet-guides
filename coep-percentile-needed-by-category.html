How Much Percentile Do You Need for COEP? Category Advantage Measured (2026)

Not sure if your percentile is enough for COEP in your category?

Real closing percentiles for all nine COEP Technological University branches from the official 2026 CAP Round 1–4 lists — and how much each category really gains over Open.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 19 Nov 2026
📄 Source: State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Quick answer

In MHT-CET CAP 2026 (official Round I–IV lists), the lowest percentile that got any COEP branch was 98.79 for Open, 98.63 for OBC, 97.22 for EWS, 95.85 for SC and 84.21 for ST. Computer Engineering closed at 99.98 (Open), 99.90 (OBC), 99.74 (EWS), 99.15 (SC) and 96.42 (ST) in Round 1.

All COEP seats in these lists are State Level seats, so your home university does not change your COEP cutoff.

Most COEP cutoff pages show only Open, or only Round 1, or still show 2025 numbers. This page puts every main category, all nine branches and all four 2026 CAP rounds side by side and measures what each category actually gains over Open. Every number below is taken from the official State CET Cell CAP 2026 cutoff lists (closing percentile, with merit rank where shown). For every seat type and the round-by-round merit numbers, open the full COEP cutoff page in our Cutoff Explorer; for the official 2026 and 2025 branch-wise lists see COEP official cutoff 2026 & 2025.

What this shows: at COEP the advantage of TFWS, OBC, girls Open, NT-D and SEBC seats is tiny — a median of 0.3 percentile or less. EWS, NT-B and NT-C gain roughly half to three-quarters of a percentile, VJ/DT and SC about 1.5–2 percentiles, and only ST seats close several percentiles lower. Plan your COEP chances with the real gap for your category, not with general “category cutoff is much lower” advice.

Girls (ladies seats) at COEP: the advantage measured

Girls compete for all general seats and for ladies seats. At COEP the ladies Open seat closed a median of 0.13 percentile below general Open in Round 1. The gap is biggest in Manufacturing (0.75) and almost zero in Computer Engineering.

TFWS at COEP: almost as hard as Open

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) seats are a small number of extra seats per branch for students whose family income is within the official limit. At COEP they are nearly as competitive as Open: in Round 1 2026 the TFWS seat closed within 0.3 percentile of Open in every branch, and above Open in 1 of 9 (Civil). So TFWS is a fee benefit at COEP, not an easier way in — always fill the normal seat too.

COEP cutoffs barely move after Round 1 — in Civil, Metallurgy and Manufacturing the last round even closed higher than Round 1. If you are more than a few tenths of a percentile below the Round 1 cutoff for your category, do not count on later rounds for COEP.

Home University and COEP

COEP Technological University is a state university. In the 2026 cutoff lists all its seats appear as State Level seats (codes ending in “S”, e.g. GOPENS). There is no Home University / Other-than-HU split at COEP, so a student from Nagpur and a student from Pune compete on the same cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

What is the COEP cutoff 2026 for OBC?

In CAP Round 1 2026, COEP Computer Engineering closed at 99.90 percentile for OBC (State Level). The lowest OBC percentile that got any COEP branch in 2026 was 98.63 (Metallurgy, Round 1).

What percentile is needed for COEP for SC and ST students?

In 2026 the lowest closing percentile at COEP was 95.85 for SC and 84.21 for ST. Computer Engineering closed at 99.15 (SC) and 96.42 (ST) in Round 1.

Is the COEP cutoff lower for girls?

Slightly. The ladies Open seat closed a median of 0.13 percentile below general Open in Round 1 2026, with the biggest gap in Manufacturing and almost no gap in Computer Engineering.

Is TFWS easier than Open at COEP?

No. In Round 1 2026 the TFWS seat at COEP closed within 0.3 percentile of Open in every branch, and above Open in Civil. TFWS is a fee benefit, not an easier entry.

Does Home University matter for COEP?

No. All COEP seats in the 2026 cutoff lists are State Level seats, so students from every district compete on the same cutoff.
