Frequently asked questions

When is the MHT-CET 2027 exam?

As per the State CET Cell's tentative schedule announced on 16 November 2026, MHT-CET 2027 PCM is on 16–26 April 2027 (Attempt 1) and 10–15 May 2027 (Attempt 2). PCB is on 8–13 April and 6–8 May 2027. Final dates come with the official notification on cetcell.mahacet.org.

Has MHT-CET 2027 registration started?

Not yet. The CET Cell has not announced registration dates for MHT-CET 2027. This page is updated the day it opens.

Where do these updates come from?

Only from official notices of the State CET Cell, Maharashtra (cetcell.mahacet.org) and DTE Maharashtra. Each update links to its source.
