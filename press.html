Data facts you can quote (2026–27)

#FactSource / full data
1JEE Main 2027 Session 1 had 13,92,344 fee-paid candidates by 23 Nov 2026 — already about 3.1% more than the 13,50,969 scheduled for Session 1 in 2026. More candidates for the same seats push percentile cutoffs up.NTA · analysis
2With 10% more MHT-CET candidates and the same seats, a college that closed at 98.13 percentile (DY Patil Akurdi Computer) would close near 98.30 — but one that closed at 89.90 would rise by about 0.92. Mid-range colleges feel competition growth most, not COEP or VJTI.CET Cell CAP 2026 · table
3The JEE Main qualifying cutoff for JEE Advanced rose in every category from 2025 to 2026 — General 93.10 → 93.41, ST up by 4.11 percentile.NTA · analysis
4At DY Patil College of Engineering, Akurdi, OBC students closed only 0.36 percentile below Open (median, Round 1 2026), while EWS closed 3.24 below. TFWS seats closed at or above Open in 6 of 8 branches.CET Cell CAP 2026 · guide
5Cutoffs do not always fall after Round 1: at DY Patil Akurdi the Open cutoff for Instrumentation was 1.72 percentile higher by Round 4 than in Round 1.CET Cell CAP 2026 · table
6MHT-CET 2026 PCM: 4,79,332 registered for Attempt 1 (4,54,069 appeared) and 3,21,918 for Attempt 2 (2,85,121 appeared).CET Cell press notes
7In 2026, JoSAA's final round (16 July) ended before the MHT-CET CAP Round 1 option form opened (28–30 July) — students in both had to decide on a JoSAA seat before knowing their CAP result.JoSAA, CET Cell · timeline
