JoSAA 2027 Counselling: Dates, Rounds and the JoSAA vs MHT-CET CAP Timeline

Writing JEE Main and MHT-CET both? Worried JoSAA and CAP dates will clash?

Official JEE and JoSAA dates in one place, the 2026 JoSAA schedule for planning, and what Maharashtra students in both JoSAA and CAP must decide first.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 24 Nov 2026
📄 Source: NTA, JEE Main 2027 · JEE Advanced 2027 (IIT Delhi) · JoSAA 2026 schedule of events · State CET Cell, MHT-CET 2026 press notes and CAP 2026 schedule✅ Free · no login
Quick answer

JoSAA 2027 dates are not announced yet. What is official so far: JEE Main 2027 Session 1 is on 20–28 January 2027 (registration closes 26 November 2026 with late fee), and JEE Advanced 2027 is on Sunday, 23 May 2027, conducted by IIT Delhi. In 2026, JoSAA registration opened the day after the JEE Advanced result (2 June) and the first seat allotment came on 13 June. So JoSAA 2027 can be expected in early June 2027 — this is an estimate based on the 2026 pattern, not an official date.

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) runs one common counselling for IITs, NITs, IIITs and other government-funded technical institutes (GFTIs). You register once, fill one list of choices, and seats are given round by round on the basis of your JEE Main rank (NITs, IIITs, GFTIs) and JEE Advanced rank (IITs). This page collects only official dates and rules, and adds one thing most JoSAA pages leave out: how JoSAA overlaps with Maharashtra's MHT-CET CAP, for students who are in both.

How JoSAA rounds work: Freeze, Float, Slide

After each round, a student who gets a seat pays the seat acceptance fee, uploads documents and chooses one option:

  • Freeze — you accept this seat and leave further rounds.
  • Float — you keep this seat but want any higher choice (any institute) in later rounds.
  • Slide — you keep this seat but want only a higher branch in the same institute.

If you get a higher choice in a later round, your earlier seat is cancelled automatically. If you do not pay the fee or report on time, the seat is lost and you are out of JoSAA. This is the same rule as “betterment” in MHT-CET CAP — read freeze or betterment in CAP for how it works in Maharashtra.

JoSAA vs MHT-CET CAP: the timeline clash for Maharashtra students

Many Maharashtra students write JEE Main and MHT-CET, so they can be in JoSAA and CAP the same year. In 2026, CAP registration ran alongside the JoSAA rounds, but JoSAA's final round was over before the CAP Round 1 option form even opened.

What this means for you:

  • You will have to decide on your JoSAA seat before you know your CAP allotment. Know your MHT-CET percentile and check last year's CAP cutoffs for your category before JoSAA Round 1, so you can compare an NIT/IIIT seat with what you can realistically get in COEP, VJTI, PICT or VIT. Use the Cutoff Explorer for this.
  • Do both registrations. Registering for CAP costs little and keeps the Maharashtra option open if you leave JoSAA.
  • Read the refund and withdrawal rules of both before paying fees: JoSAA and CAP have separate fee and cancellation rules.
  • Admission to Maharashtra colleges through JEE Main (All India seats) is a separate route inside CAP — see engineering admission through JEE Main in Maharashtra.

Documents to keep ready for JoSAA

  • JEE Main 2027 admit card and score card; JEE Advanced admit card (for IITs).
  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets (Class 10 for date of birth).
  • Category certificate in the format JoSAA asks for: OBC-NCL (central list), EWS, SC/ST, PwD — issued in the correct financial year.
  • Photo ID, passport-size photos, and the medical fitness certificate format given in the JoSAA business rules.

Maharashtra OBC-NCL and EWS certificates for central admissions must follow the central format — a state-format certificate used for MHT-CET CAP may not be accepted in JoSAA. Check the JoSAA business rules for 2027 when they come out.

Frequently asked questions

When will JoSAA 2027 counselling start?

JoSAA 2027 dates are not announced yet. JEE Advanced 2027 is on 23 May 2027. In 2026, JoSAA registration started on 2 June, the day after the JEE Advanced result, so JoSAA 2027 can be expected in early June 2027 (estimate).

How many rounds are there in JoSAA?

JoSAA 2026 ran its rounds from 13 June to a final round on 16 July 2026. After JoSAA, vacant NIT, IIIT and GFTI seats are filled through CSAB special rounds.

What is the JEE Advanced 2027 exam date?

JEE Advanced 2027 is on Sunday, 23 May 2027, conducted by IIT Delhi. The top 2,50,000 JEE Main 2027 candidates can appear, and an on-screen basic calculator will be allowed.

What is freeze, float and slide in JoSAA?

Freeze: accept the seat and leave further rounds. Float: keep the seat but take any higher choice in later rounds. Slide: keep the seat but take only a higher branch in the same institute.

Can I take part in JoSAA and MHT-CET CAP in the same year?

Yes. In 2026 JoSAA finished (final round 16 July) before the CAP Round 1 option form (28–30 July), so you have to decide on a JoSAA seat before you know your CAP result. Register for both.

Is my Maharashtra OBC/EWS certificate valid for JoSAA?

JoSAA follows central formats (OBC-NCL central list, central EWS format). A state-format certificate used for MHT-CET CAP may not be accepted. Check the JoSAA business rules for the year.
