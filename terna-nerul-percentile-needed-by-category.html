How Much Percentile Do You Need for Terna Engineering College, Nerul? Category Advantage Measured (2026)

Not sure if your percentile is enough for Terna Nerul in your category?

Real closing percentiles for all 8 Terna Nerul branches from the official 2026 CAP Round 1–4 lists, and how much each category really gains over Open.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 23 Nov 2026
📄 Source: State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Yash's answer

If you get Terna in Round 1 and still want to try for something better, take betterment and pay the seat acceptance fee. Your Terna seat is safe until you get a new seat that you placed above Terna in your options. So in Round 2 put above Terna only the colleges and branches you would truly prefer. If you refill everything, including options below Terna, and one of them is allotted, your Terna seat is cancelled automatically — CET Cell does not ask which seat you want.

Quick answer (2026 data)

In MHT-CET CAP 2026 (official Round I–IV lists), Terna Engineering College, Nerul Computer Engineering closed at 93.45 for Open, 92.21 for OBC, 81.93 for EWS, 89.53 for SC and 66.09 for ST in Round 1. The lowest Open percentile that got any Terna branch was 84.54 (Electronics, Round 2).

All Terna Nerul CAP seats in these lists are State Level seats — Mumbai University or not, every student competes on the same cutoff.

Terna Engineering College (Nerul, Navi Mumbai, college code 3190) has 8 branches in CAP. Most Terna cutoff pages show only Open, an “expected” number, or one round. This page puts every main category, all 8 branches and all four 2026 CAP rounds side by side and measures how much each category actually gains over Open. Every number is from the official State CET Cell CAP 2026 cutoff lists. For every seat type (defence, PwD, orphan) and merit ranks, open the full Terna Nerul page in our Cutoff Explorer.

TFWS at Terna: about as hard as Open

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) seats are a few extra seats per branch for students whose family income is within the official limit. At Terna the TFWS seat closed at or above the Open cutoff in 4 of 8 branches in Round 1 2026 (median +0.26 percentile vs Open). In Computer Engineering TFWS closed at 94.34 against 93.45 for Open. So apply for TFWS if you are eligible, but always keep the normal seat in your option form too.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Terna Engineering College Nerul cutoff 2026 for Computer Engineering?

In CAP Round 1 2026, Terna Nerul Computer Engineering closed at 93.45 percentile for Open, 92.21 for OBC, 81.93 for EWS and 89.53 for SC (State Level seats).

What is the Terna Nerul cutoff for OBC?

The lowest OBC percentile that got any Terna Nerul branch in 2026 was 76.80 (Civil, Round 2). In Round 1, OBC closed a median of 2.86 percentile below Open.

How much percentile is needed for Terna for SC and ST students?

In 2026 the lowest closing percentile at Terna Nerul was 72.85 for SC and 49.63 for ST. Computer Engineering closed at 89.53 (SC) and 66.09 (ST) in Round 1.

Is the Terna cutoff lower for girls?

Slightly, in most branches. The ladies Open seat closed a median of 0.46 percentile below general Open in Round 1 2026, with the biggest gap in Mechanical (6.87).

Is TFWS easier than Open at Terna?

Not usually. In Round 1 2026 the TFWS seat closed at or above Open in 4 of 8 Terna Nerul branches; in Computer Engineering TFWS closed at 94.34 against 93.45 for Open.

Does Home University (Mumbai University) matter for Terna Nerul?

No. All Terna Nerul seats in the 2026 CAP cutoff lists are State Level seats, so students from every district compete on the same cutoff.

Do Terna cutoffs fall in later CAP rounds?

Not reliably. The biggest Open dip after Round 1 in 2026 was 2.98 percentile (Mechanical), and in 5 branches the cutoff was higher in a later round than in Round 1.

I got Terna in Round 1. Should I freeze or take betterment?

Yash Aradhye advises doing betterment and paying the seat acceptance fee if you still want something better: your seat is safe until you get a seat you placed above it. In Round 2 put above Terna only options you would truly prefer; if an option you placed there is allotted, the Terna seat is cancelled.
