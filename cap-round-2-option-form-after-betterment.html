CAP Round 2 Option Form After Betterment: Only Better Options

Worried that the Round 2 option form could cost you the seat you already have?

Why students lose a good seat in Round 2, and Yash Aradhye's simple method to fill the Round 2 and Round 3 option forms.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: Yash Aradhye videos · State CET Cell CAP 2026 lists✅ Free · no login

After betterment, which options should I fill for CAP Round 2?

Only options that are truly better than the seat you already have. In Round 1 fill the maximum options; in Round 2 fill the minimum. If you add a college worse than your current seat and it gets allotted, your better Round 1 seat is cancelled automatically.

Answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET admission counsellor, Baner, Pune — 7 years of MHT-CET counselling (since 2020). Explained in his videos: freeze or betterment, step by step and why seats get cancelled even after betterment.

Silver, gold and iron: the simplest way to understand it

Think of your Round 1 seat as silver. In Round 2, CET Cell asks you only one thing: “what do you want that is better than silver?” — and you say gold. If you also put iron in your list, CET Cell will give you iron too if it is available — and your silver is gone. Whoever has gold should not ask for iron.

Example: you got VIT Computer in Round 1

The logical Round 2 list has only two or three options: COEP, PICT, Walchand — and Cummins for a girl. Nothing else is needed. But many students re-enter the same long Round 1 list. If that list has a college below VIT (say DY Patil) and DY Patil gets allotted, the VIT seat is cancelled automatically. CET Cell will not ask which seat you want.

The 3-step method for your Round 2 list

  1. Your current seat goes last. Even if you don't add it, your allotted option sits automatically at the bottom of your list. Everything you put above it must be better.
  2. Remove what you no longer want. Say you filled 100 options and got option 50. Look at the 49 options above it. If any of them is no longer better for you now (for example, you got CS and an IT option in the same college is above it), delete it.
  3. Lift only the really better ones from below. Look at the options that were below 50 (keep last round's PDF saved — they won't show on the portal). If one is actually better for you (for example Walchand IT you had kept low), you can move it up. But if you lift everything, you will lose your seat.
Rule of thumb: Round 1 — maximum options and backups. Round 2 onwards — minimum options, only upgrades. Fill the form in the first two days; don't wait for the last moment.

Frequently asked questions

Which options should I fill in CAP Round 2 after betterment?

Only options that are truly better than your current seat. Answer by Yash Aradhye: in Round 1 fill maximum options, in Round 2 minimum — anything worse than your seat can get allotted and cancel your better seat.

If I get a worse college in Round 2, can I go back to my Round 1 seat?

No. Any allotment in Round 2 automatically cancels your previous seat. CET Cell does not let you choose between the two.

Do I need to add my current college again in the Round 2 list?

No. Your allotted option automatically stays at the bottom of your list. Put only better options above it.

Should I fill the same long list in Round 2 as in Round 1?

No. Re-entering the whole Round 1 list is the most common way to lose a good seat. Remove every option you would not prefer over your current seat.

I got CS in a college. Should I keep IT of the same college in Round 2?

If you would not prefer it over your current CS seat, remove it. Anything you keep above your seat may be allotted and replace it.

Can I move an option from lower in my list to higher in Round 2?

Yes, if it is genuinely better for you than your current seat. Keep last round's PDF saved to see the options that were below your allotment, because the portal may not show them.

How many options should I fill in Round 2?

As few as needed — often two or three for a student who already has a good college. The number doesn't matter; only that each one is an upgrade you would be happy with.

When should I fill the Round 2 and Round 3 option forms?

In the first two days. Don't wait for the last moment as many students did in Round 1 — the portal gets slow and mistakes happen.

Does CET Cell ask me before cancelling my old seat?

No. If an option above your current seat is allotted, the old seat is cancelled by default.

Will the cutoff of my dream college drop enough in Round 2?

For top colleges, usually very little. In 2026 the median Round 1→2 drop was 0.02 percentile for colleges that closed at 99+ in Round 1.
