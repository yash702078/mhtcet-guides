MHT-CET 2027 Registration: Date, Fees and Last Date (Status Today)

Has MHT-CET 2027 registration started? Am I going to miss the date?

Is the MHT-CET 2027 form open? Official status, last year’s exact dates, extensions and fees, and what to prepare now.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 25 Nov 2026
📄 Source: CET Cell tentative schedule for CETs 2027-28 (16 Nov 2026) · CET Cell MHT-CET 2026 registration final extension notice · CET Cell MHT-CET 2026 late-fee registration notice · MHT-CET 2026 information brochure (fees)✅ Free · no login
Status (25 Nov 2026)

MHT-CET 2027 registration is not open yet. CET Cell has announced only the tentative exam dates (16 Nov 2026): PCM Attempt 1 16–26 April 2027, Attempt 2 10–15 May 2027; PCB 8–13 April and 6–8 May 2027 (official schedule PDF). The registration dates, fee and brochure for 2027 have not been published. This page will be updated the same day they are.

Last year the gap between the tentative schedule (21 Nov 2025) and the start of registration (10 Jan 2026) was about seven weeks. If CET Cell follows the same pattern, 2027 registration would open around early January 2027. This is our estimate from the 2026 pattern, not an official date.

Yash's answer

Register for both attempts. Yash Aradhye advises his students to give both MHT-CET attempts: the second attempt is a second chance at a better percentile, and the higher of the two counts.

MHT-CET registration dates: 2026 (official) and 2027 (status)

The order of steps stays the same every year. In 2026 the registration deadline was extended, and there were two late-fee windows. Many students miss these windows, so note how they worked.

MHT-CET registration fee

The 2027 fee is not announced. Some websites already show an “MHT-CET 2027 fee” — that is only the 2026 fee repeated, and the 2027 fee can be different. Here is the official 2026 fee from the information brochure; it is non-refundable.

Category (2026)One attemptBoth attempts
General (Open) and candidates from outside Maharashtra₹1,300₹2,600
Reserved categories of Maharashtra, PwD, orphan, transgender₹1,000₹2,000

Source: MHT-CET 2026 information brochure (fee section). Late fee in 2026: ₹500 extra.

One attempt or both? What the 2026 numbers show

In 2026, 479,332 students registered for PCM Attempt 1 and 454,069 appeared. For Attempt 2, 321,918 registered and 285,121 appeared (CET Cell press notes, June 2026). So the number of Attempt 2 registrations was about 67% of Attempt 1 — a large share of students chose to try twice.

For 2027 the CET Cell schedule again has two attempts, both after the Class 12 board exams. Your better percentile is used for CAP. Choosing both attempts at registration costs more (in 2026: ₹2,600 instead of ₹1,300 for Open), so decide at the time of filling the form.

What to do now, before registration opens

  • Start your certificates now. The CET registration form itself is short, but for CAP admission later you need category documents (caste certificate, caste validity, non-creamy layer, EWS, domicile). Some take weeks. Check the list for your category: engineering admission documents, category-wise.
  • Use one mobile number and email that you will keep until CAP ends — all OTPs and allotment messages come there.
  • Get the link the day it opens: join the free WhatsApp group below; we post the official link and every extension notice.

मराठीत थोडक्यात

MHT-CET 2027 चे registration अजून सुरू झालेले नाही (25 Nov 2026). CET Cell ने फक्त परीक्षेच्या तात्पुरत्या तारखा जाहीर केल्या आहेत: PCM Attempt 1 16–26 एप्रिल 2027, Attempt 2 10–15 मे 2027. 2026 मध्ये registration 10 जानेवारीला सुरू झाले होते, म्हणून 2027 मध्ये ते जानेवारीच्या सुरुवातीला सुरू होऊ शकते — हा आमचा अंदाज आहे, अधिकृत तारीख नाही. 2026 ची फी: Open ₹1,300 (एक attempt) / ₹2,600 (दोन्ही); महाराष्ट्रातील राखीव प्रवर्ग ₹1,000 / ₹2,000. यश आराध्ये यांचा सल्ला: दोन्ही attempts द्या. जात वैधता, नॉन-क्रिमी लेयर, EWS, डोमिसाइल ही प्रमाणपत्रे आत्तापासूनच काढायला सुरुवात करा.

Frequently asked questions

When will MHT-CET 2027 registration start?

Not announced yet (checked 25 Nov 2026). In 2026 registration opened on 10 January, about seven weeks after the tentative schedule; the 2027 schedule came on 16 November 2026, so early January 2027 is likely — this is an estimate, not an official date.

What is the last date for MHT-CET 2027 registration?

Not announced. In 2026 the last date was first 12 February, extended to 24 February, followed by a late-fee window (25–27 February) and a special window (19–20 March), each with ₹500 late fee.

What is the MHT-CET 2027 registration fee?

Not announced. The official 2026 fee was ₹1,300 for one attempt and ₹2,600 for both (Open and outside-Maharashtra candidates), and ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 for Maharashtra reserved categories, PwD, orphan and transgender candidates.

Should I register for both MHT-CET attempts in 2027?

Yash Aradhye advises giving both attempts: the second attempt is a second chance and the higher percentile counts. In 2026, Attempt 2 registrations were about two-thirds of Attempt 1 registrations.

When is the MHT-CET 2027 exam?

PCM Attempt 1 16–26 April 2027 and Attempt 2 10–15 May 2027; PCB 8–13 April and 6–8 May 2027 (CET Cell tentative schedule, 16 November 2026).

Where do I register for MHT-CET 2027?

Only on the official State CET Cell website, cetcell.mahacet.org, when the registration link is published. Do not pay on any other website.
