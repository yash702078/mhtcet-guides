How Much Percentile Do You Need for DY Patil College of Engineering, Akurdi? Category Advantage Measured (2026)

Not sure if your percentile is enough for DY Patil Akurdi in your category?

Real closing percentiles for all 8 DY Patil Akurdi branches from the official 2026 CAP Round 1–4 lists, and how much each category really gains over Open.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 24 Nov 2026
📄 Source: State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Yash's answer

If you get DY Patil Akurdi in Round 1 and still want to try for something better, take betterment and pay the seat acceptance fee. Your DY Patil Akurdi seat is safe until you get a new seat that you placed above DY Patil Akurdi in your options. So in Round 2 put above DY Patil Akurdi only the colleges and branches you would truly prefer. If you refill everything, including options below DY Patil Akurdi, and one of them is allotted, your DY Patil Akurdi seat is cancelled automatically — CET Cell does not ask which seat you want.

Quick answer (2026 data)

In MHT-CET CAP 2026 (official Round I–IV lists), DY Patil Akurdi Computer Engineering closed at 98.13 for Open, 97.99 for OBC, 96.30 for EWS, 94.78 for SC and 80.78 for ST in Round 1. The lowest Open percentile that got any DY Patil Akurdi branch was 94.42 (Instrumentation, Round 1).

DY Patil Akurdi is an autonomous institute, and all its CAP seats in these lists are State Level seats — Pune University area or not, every student competes on the same cutoff.

Dr. D. Y. Patil College of Engineering, Akurdi (DYPCOE, Pimpri-Chinchwad, college code 6272) has 8 branches in CAP. Most DY Patil Akurdi cutoff pages show only Open, an “expected” number, or one round. This page puts every main category, all 8 branches and all four 2026 CAP rounds side by side and measures how much each category actually gains over Open. Every number is from the official State CET Cell CAP 2026 cutoff lists. TFWS at DY Patil Akurdi: harder than Open

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) seats are a few extra seats per branch for students whose family income is within the official limit. At DY Patil Akurdi the TFWS seat closed at or above the Open cutoff in 6 of 8 branches in Round 1 2026 (median +0.29 percentile vs Open). In Computer Engineering TFWS closed at 98.41 against 98.13 for Open. So apply for TFWS if you are eligible, but always keep the normal seat in your option form too.

Frequently asked questions

What is the DY Patil College of Engineering Akurdi cutoff 2026 for Computer Engineering?

In CAP Round 1 2026, DY Patil Akurdi Computer Engineering closed at 98.13 percentile for Open, 97.99 for OBC, 96.30 for EWS and 94.78 for SC (State Level seats).

What is the DY Patil Akurdi cutoff for OBC?

The lowest OBC percentile that got any DY Patil Akurdi branch in 2026 was 93.76 (Civil, Round 1). In Round 1, OBC closed a median of 0.36 percentile below Open.

How much percentile is needed for DY Patil Akurdi for SC and ST students?

In 2026 the lowest closing percentile at DY Patil Akurdi was 87.12 for SC and 24.78 for ST. Computer Engineering closed at 94.78 (SC) and 80.78 (ST) in Round 1.

Is the DY Patil Akurdi cutoff lower for girls?

Slightly, in most branches. The ladies Open seat closed a median of 0.24 percentile below general Open in Round 1 2026, with the biggest gap in Mechanical (1.07).

Is TFWS easier than Open at DY Patil Akurdi?

Not usually. In Round 1 2026 the TFWS seat closed at or above Open in 6 of 8 DY Patil Akurdi branches; in Computer Engineering TFWS closed at 98.41 against 98.13 for Open.

Does Home University (Pune University) matter for DY Patil Akurdi?

No. DY Patil Akurdi is an autonomous institute and all its seats in the 2026 CAP cutoff lists are State Level seats, so students from every district compete on the same cutoff.

Do DY Patil Akurdi cutoffs fall in later CAP rounds?

Not reliably. The biggest Open dip after Round 1 in 2026 was 0.48 percentile (Robotics & Automation), and in 3 branches the cutoff was higher in a later round than in Round 1.

I got DY Patil Akurdi in Round 1. Should I freeze or take betterment?

Yash Aradhye advises doing betterment and paying the seat acceptance fee if you still want something better: your seat is safe until you get a seat you placed above it. In Round 2 put above DY Patil Akurdi only options you would truly prefer; if an option you placed there is allotted, the DY Patil Akurdi seat is cancelled.
