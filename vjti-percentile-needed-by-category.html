How Much Percentile Do You Need for VJTI? Category Advantage Measured (2026)

Not sure if your percentile is enough for VJTI in your category?

Real closing percentiles for all nine VJTI Mumbai branches from the official 2026 CAP Round 1–4 lists — and how much each category really gains over Open.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I–IV cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Quick answer

In MHT-CET CAP 2026 (official Round I–IV lists), the lowest percentile that got any VJTI branch was 97.98 for Open, 96.59 for OBC, 95.11 for EWS, 94.22 for SC and 72.53 for ST — every one of these was in Textile. Computer Engineering closed at 99.99 (Open), 99.98 (OBC), 99.86 (EWS), 99.39 (SC) and 98.14 (ST) in Round 1.

All VJTI seats in these lists are State Level seats, so it does not matter which district or university area you are from.

VJTI (Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute, Matunga, Mumbai) is one of the two colleges most MHT-CET toppers aim for. Most VJTI cutoff pages show only Open or only Round 1. This page puts every main category, all nine branches and all four 2026 CAP rounds side by side and measures how much each category actually gains over Open. Every number is from the official State CET Cell CAP 2026 cutoff lists. For Round 1 branch-wise cutoffs read our VJTI cutoff 2026 (CAP Round 1); for every seat type (including defence, PwD and orphan seats) and merit ranks, open the full VJTI cutoff page in our Cutoff Explorer.

What this shows: at VJTI the median gain is under half a percentile for TFWS, Girls Open, OBC, SEBC; between half and two percentiles for NT-D, Girls OBC, NT-C, EWS, NT-B, VJ/DT; and two percentiles or more only for SC, ST. The gaps are much bigger in the easier branches (Textile, Production, Civil) than in Computer or IT, where every category except ST closed at 99.04 or above in Round 1. Use the gap for your own category and branch, not general “reserved cutoff is much lower” advice.

TFWS at VJTI: not an easier way in

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) seats are a few extra seats per branch for students whose family income is within the official limit. At VJTI, the TFWS seat closed at most 0.86 percentile below Open in Round 1 2026. So at VJTI TFWS is a fee benefit, not a lower cutoff — always keep the normal seat in your option form too.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VJTI cutoff 2026 for OBC?

In CAP Round 1 2026, VJTI Computer Engineering closed at 99.98 percentile for OBC (State Level). The lowest OBC percentile that got any VJTI branch in 2026 was 96.59 (Textile, Round 4).

How much percentile is needed for VJTI for SC and ST students?

In 2026 the lowest closing percentile at VJTI was 94.22 for SC and 72.53 for ST. Computer Engineering closed at 99.39 (SC) and 98.14 (ST) in Round 1.

Is the VJTI cutoff lower for girls?

Slightly. The ladies Open seat closed a median of 0.18 percentile below general Open in Round 1 2026, with the biggest gap in Textile (0.86).

Is TFWS easier than Open at VJTI?

No. In Round 1 2026 the TFWS seat at VJTI closed at most 0.86 percentile below Open in any branch. TFWS is a fee benefit, not an easier entry.

Does Home University matter for VJTI?

No. All VJTI seats in the 2026 CAP cutoff lists are State Level seats, so students from every district compete on the same cutoff.
