Frequently asked questions

Is the 10-minute consultation really free?

Yes. There is no fee and no obligation to buy a counselling plan. It is a free 10-minute expert guidance call on Google Meet.

Who will talk to me on the call?

A counsellor from Yash Aradhye's MHT-CET counselling team, on Google Meet.

How do I join the Google Meet?

After booking, the Google Meet link comes to the email you entered, as a Google Calendar invite. Open it on your phone or laptop at your slot time; the Google Meet app or a browser both work.

Can my parents join?

Yes. Parents are welcome to join the same Meet link with the student.

What if no slot suits me?

New slots open regularly. If none suits you, message us on WhatsApp at +91 81492 05305 and the team will reply.

Can I book more than once?

One free consultation per student is the idea, so that more students get a chance. For complete help through the CAP rounds, see the Saarthi counselling plans.
