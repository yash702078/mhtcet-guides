Freeze or Betterment: If I Get Nothing, Do I Lose My Seat? No.

Expert answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET counsellor in Pune: when your CAP seat is safe, when it is lost, and the exact steps on the portal.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 21 Nov 2026
📄 Source: Yash Aradhye videos · State CET Cell CAP 2026 lists✅ Free · no login

If I choose betterment and get nothing in the next round, do I lose my Round 1 seat?

No. Your seat is safe until you get a new seat from the options you put above your allotted seat — provided you chose betterment and paid the seat acceptance fee. If nothing better is allotted, you keep the same college and the same branch.

Answer by Yash Aradhye, MHT-CET admission counsellor, Baner, Pune — 7 years of MHT-CET counselling (since 2020). Explained in his videos: freeze or betterment, step by step and why seats get cancelled even after betterment.

The two ways students lose a good seat every year: (1) choosing betterment but not paying the seat acceptance fee — the seat is cancelled automatically; (2) clicking “Incorrect” during self-verification. Read the steps below before you log in.

Yash Aradhye's advice: choose betterment (and pay the fee)

My suggestion to almost every student is betterment, because betterment does not cause any loss — your seat stays with you while you try for something better. The only real exception: if you have got the college you truly want and you are happy to join it, freeze it and report.

The seat acceptance fee (₹1,000 in recent CAP years — check the amount in the 2027 notice) is a one-time payment. Some students think “I don't want this college, why pay?” and skip it. That is the mistake: without the fee the seat is cancelled. And you cannot avoid the fee anyway — if you don't pay it in Round 1, it is taken in Round 2, 3 or 4 whenever you accept a seat.

Step by step on the CAP portal (after the allotment)

  1. Log in and open your allotment. You see your CET, SSC and HSC details and your allotted institute. Click Proceed to application form.
  2. Self-verification: click “Correct” on every row. The portal is only asking whether the information you gave is correct — gender, candidature type, category, EWS, PwD, orphan, TFWS yes/no. It is not asking whether you got a TFWS or EWS seat. If you had said TFWS “Yes”, it shows Yes — that is correct. Do not change anything.
  3. HSC marks and documents: these are what you submitted and what is in the merit list. Every document should show Verified. If anything shows “Not accepted”, sort it out before you continue.
  4. Save and proceed. This is your self-confirmation that all your information is correct.
  5. Choose Betterment (green) or Freeze. Enter your password and the OTP to confirm.
  6. Pay the seat acceptance fee online (select payment mode, save and proceed).
  7. Check the status: both must show the right thing — “Betterment” and “Confirmed”. If it shows “Not confirmed”, the fee is not paid and your Round 1 seat will be cancelled.

If you froze: report to the allotted college with all documents within the dates on the official website. If you chose betterment: no college visit is needed now — wait for the next round.

When is my seat safe — and when is it lost?

SituationYour Round 1 seat

CET Cell never gives you two allotments to choose from. At any time you hold one allotment. That is why the options you fill for the next round matter so much — see CAP Round 2 option form after betterment.

Will the cutoff drop in the next round? (real 2026 data)

From the official 2026 CAP lists for 2,327 Open-category seats: for colleges that closed at 99+ percentile in Round 1, the median drop in Round 2 was only 0.02 percentile; for 97–99 it was 0.01. Lower down the list it moved more — but the last round actually closed higher than Round 1 for 71% of seats below 80. So don't give up a good seat hoping the cutoff will fall. Frequently asked questions

If I choose betterment and get nothing, do I lose my Round 1 seat?

No. Answer by Yash Aradhye: your seat is safe until you get a new seat from the options you placed above your allotted seat — provided you chose betterment and paid the seat acceptance fee. If nothing better is allotted, you keep the same college and branch.

Freeze or betterment — which should I choose?

Yash Aradhye advises betterment for almost every student, because it causes no loss: your seat stays with you while you try for better. Freeze only if you have got the college you really want and will join it.

What is the difference between freeze and betterment?

Freeze means you accept the allotted seat and stop; you report to the college within the given days. Betterment means you keep the seat and take part in the next CAP round, where only options above your current seat are tried.

Do I need to pay the seat acceptance fee if I choose betterment?

Yes. Without the seat acceptance fee your current seat is cancelled automatically, even if you selected betterment. Paying it is what keeps the seat safe.

How much is the seat acceptance fee?

₹1,000 in recent CAP years, paid online. Check the exact amount in the official 2027 CAP notice.

Do I have to pay the seat acceptance fee again in every round?

No. It is a one-time payment. If you don't pay it in Round 1, it is collected in Round 2, 3 or 4 whenever you accept a seat — you cannot avoid it, so pay it in time.

I don't like my Round 1 college. Should I still pay ₹1,000?

Yes, if you choose betterment. The fee keeps your current seat as a safety net. If you skip it, the seat is cancelled and you could end with nothing better.

What happens to my seat if I get a better option in Round 2?

The new seat is allotted and your old seat is cancelled automatically. CET Cell does not ask you which one you want — you hold only one allotment at a time.

Can CET Cell give me two allotments and let me choose?

No. At any time you hold one allotment. That is why you should only fill options in the next round that you would truly prefer over your current seat.

What should I click in self-verification?

Click “Correct” on every row. The portal only asks whether the information you gave (gender, candidature type, category, EWS, TFWS yes/no, PwD, orphan, HSC marks) is correct. Clicking “Incorrect” can change your merit or cancel your seat.

The portal shows TFWS “Yes” but I didn't get a TFWS seat. Should I click Incorrect?

No. The portal is asking whether you had selected TFWS “Yes” in your application, not whether you got a TFWS seat. If you selected Yes, it is correct — click Correct.

What if a document shows “Not accepted”?

Every document should show Verified. If one shows “Not accepted”, it can cause a problem with your seat — sort it out before you proceed.

How do I know my betterment is done?

The status must show “Betterment” and “Confirmed”. “Not confirmed” means the seat acceptance fee is not paid, and your Round 1 seat will be cancelled.

Do I need to visit the college if I choose betterment?

No. With betterment you don't need to visit any college now. With freeze you must report to the college with all documents within the dates given on the official website (about three days).

What if I got my first preference?

Then there is no option above your seat to try for, so betterment has nothing to give you. Accept the seat, pay the fee and report to the college as per the schedule.

Why do students lose their seat even after choosing betterment?

Two reasons Yash Aradhye sees every year: they did not pay the seat acceptance fee, or in the next round they filled options worse than their current seat (and one of those got allotted, which automatically cancels the old seat).

Will the cutoff drop in Round 2?

Usually a little. In the 2026 CAP lists the median drop from Round 1 to Round 2 was 0.02 percentile for colleges that closed at 99+ and 0.01 for 97–99. Lower-ranked colleges moved more, and many closed higher in the last round than in Round 1.

Does betterment affect my scholarship?

No — a CAP seat keeps its scholarship eligibility. Only spot round / Against-CAP admissions get no Government scholarship.

Is there betterment after Round 4?

After the CAP rounds come institute-level and spot rounds, which are different. Read our spot round and scholarship guide before you leave a CAP seat.

What if I miss the betterment/freeze deadline?

If you don't complete the steps and pay the seat acceptance fee within the given time, the allotment is treated as rejected and the seat is cancelled. Do it in the first day or two, not at the last minute.
