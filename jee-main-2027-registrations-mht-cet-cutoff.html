JEE Main 2027 Registrations Cross 15.69 Lakh: What It Means for MHT-CET 2027 Cutoffs

15.69 lakh students applied for JEE Main 2027 — will MHT-CET cutoffs go up this year?

Official registration trend for JEE Main and MHT-CET, why more candidates push percentile cutoffs up, and an estimate for your target college from our 2026 CAP data.

By Yash Aradhye · MHT-CET counsellor, Baner, Pune·Updated 24 Nov 2026
📄 Source: NTA, JEE Main 2027 Session 1 registration update (23 Nov 2026) · State CET Cell, Press note MHT-CET PCM 1st attempt 2026 · State CET Cell, Press note MHT-CET PCM 2nd attempt 2026 · State CET Cell CAP 2026 Round I cutoff lists✅ Free · no login
Yash's answer

If more students register this year than last year, cutoffs will go up and getting a college becomes more difficult. If fewer students register, admission can become a little easier.

Yash Aradhye, 24 Nov 2026

Quick answer (official numbers)

NTA says 15,69,514 registration forms have been submitted for JEE Main 2027 Session 1, and 13,92,344 candidates have paid the fee (NTA post, 23 Nov 2026). The last date with late fee is 26 November 2026. Fee-paid candidates alone are already 3.1% more than the 13,50,969 candidates scheduled for JEE Main 2026 Session 1, and there are still days left.

For MHT-CET, CET Cell's own press notes show 4,79,332 PCM candidates registered for Attempt 1 in 2026 (4,54,069 appeared). If MHT-CET 2027 also grows and seats stay about the same, the percentile needed for the same college goes up — most of all for mid-range colleges, not for COEP or VJTI.

Every year in November students ask us one question: “So many students are applying, will the cutoff go up?” This page answers it with official numbers and with our own 2026 CAP data. We show (1) how many students registered for JEE Main and MHT-CET, year by year, (2) why more candidates push the percentile cutoff up even when the rank cutoff stays the same, and (3) how much each college's cutoff could move, with a calculator for your own target college.

Registration trend: JEE Main and MHT-CET

JEE Main and MHT-CET are different exams, but they are written by the same pool of Class 12 PCM students. A big rise in JEE Main registrations is an early sign that the engineering pool is growing. MHT-CET 2027 registration has not opened yet, so JEE Main is the first real number we have.

Why more candidates push the percentile cutoff up

Seats in a college do not change much from year to year. So the last student admitted to a branch has about the same merit rank every year. But a percentile tells you what share of all candidates scored below you. With more candidates, the same rank becomes a higher percentile.

Example: in 2026 the last Open seat in DY Patil Akurdi Computer went at merit rank 8,066 and 98.13 percentile. If 10% more students write MHT-CET 2027 and seats stay the same, a student at that same rank would be at about 98.30 percentile. The cutoff “rises” by 0.17 without the college becoming any more popular.

This also works the other way: if fewer students register, the same rank means a lower percentile, and admission becomes a little easier.

What this estimate does not know

  • Seats can change. New branches or extra intake raise the closing rank and can cancel the effect.
  • Two attempts. MHT-CET 2027 again has two attempts (PCM: 16–26 April and 10–15 May 2027, tentative, CET Cell 16 Nov 2026). Percentile is per attempt and session, so a bigger pool does not hit every session the same way.
  • JEE Main is only a signal. MHT-CET 2027 registration numbers will be the real test. We will update this page when CET Cell publishes them.

Frequently asked questions

How many students registered for JEE Main 2027?

As per NTA's post of 23 November 2026, 15,69,514 registration forms were submitted for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 and 13,92,344 candidates had paid the fee. The last date with late fee is 26 November 2026.

Are JEE Main 2027 registrations higher than last year?

Yes. Fee-paid registrations (13,92,344) are already about 3.1% above the 13,50,969 candidates scheduled for JEE Main 2026 Session 1, before the late-fee window closed.

Will MHT-CET 2027 cutoffs increase?

If MHT-CET 2027 registrations also rise and seats stay about the same, percentile cutoffs will likely go up, because the same merit rank becomes a higher percentile. The rise is small for top colleges like COEP and larger for colleges that closed at 85–95 percentile.

How many students registered for MHT-CET 2026 PCM?

As per CET Cell press notes, 4,79,332 registered for PCM Attempt 1 (4,54,069 appeared) and 3,21,918 for Attempt 2 (2,85,121 appeared).

If fewer students register, is admission easier?

Yes, a little. With fewer candidates the same merit rank means a lower percentile, so percentile cutoffs can fall slightly.

Has MHT-CET 2027 registration started?

Not yet. CET Cell has announced only tentative exam dates (16 Nov 2026). This page will be updated when registration opens and when CET Cell publishes the number of applicants.
